[h1]Co-op Bots (English)[/h1]
Configurable AI teammates for the official multiplayer lobby. The host drives everything; bots play cards, pick rewards, shop and vote on the map.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]RitsuLib[/b] 0.6.2+ — hard dependency; the mod cannot load without it.
[*]Every human must run the [b]same version[/b] of CoopBots.
[*]Game version v0.111.0.
[/list]

[h2]0.37.1 changes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Fixed: with any seat handed to the AI, a human's own card choice (Survivor discard, Attack / Power Potion pick, event picks) was answered by the bot and never synced to the other peers. The auto-select hatch now only goes in when every seat is handed to the AI.
[*]Cost, stated plainly: on a mixed table the handed-over seat skips its card reward. Its other choices are unaffected.
[*]0.37.0's all-bot long search and the execution fixes are unchanged.
[/list]
[url=https://github.com/imbalilang/STS2-Co-op_Bots/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md]Full changelog[/url]

[h2]AI tiers[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Flash[/b]: 0.5 s/card, budget x0.5
[*][b]Pro[/b]: 1.5 s/card, budget x1.5
[*][b]Cheated[/b]: Pro pacing, 3x gold
[*]All three run the same complete algorithm; only pacing and budget differ.
[/list]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Joint team decisions: shared Vulnerable / Weak, per-member incoming damage, block projection, kill callouts
[*]Combat search on a [b]real simulation[/b] (CombatSolver kernel): with a human at the table it replans from the real state, in all-bot mode it searches once and follows the script
[*]Deck building by distance from the target build: rewards, shop and removals share one objective, skipping is real
[*]Combat, rewards, shop, events, camp and route advice are all handled by the AI
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Only the host runs the full logic; clients sync the results.
[*]Bots act during the human turn and finish on their own once every human has ended.
[*]Bots have fixed names (Bot 1/2/3).
[*]Isolated build, verified live in multiplayer on the real client (A10, all bots, to act 2); no win-rate claim.
[*]All-bot searches can run for tens of seconds to minutes; that depth is deliberate.
[/list]
