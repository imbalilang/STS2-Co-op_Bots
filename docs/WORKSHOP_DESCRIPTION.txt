[h1]Co-op Bots (English)[/h1]
Configurable AI teammates for the official multiplayer lobby. The host drives everything; bots play cards, pick rewards, shop and vote on the map.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]RitsuLib[/b] 0.6.2+ — hard dependency; the mod cannot load without it.
[*]Every human must run the [b]same version[/b] of CoopBots.
[*]Game version v0.111.0.
[/list]

[h2]0.39.0 changes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Mixed human/bot tables use the same roll-out tournament[/b] as all-bot tables, and the stored script is followed step by step instead of re-searched on every card — the per-step re-search was ~6 ms of the main thread every frame; that mixed-table stutter is gone. Performance tiers work on any table with a bot.
[*][b]Not playing is a real option[/b]: Wisp and a low-threat Piercing Wail are held; one-turn buffs are played before the allies they boost; temporary stat potions need matching cards left.
[*][b]Theft has a price[/b]; [b]routes read relics and gold[/b] (relics steer elites, 500+ gold heads for the nearest shop, healthy all-bot teams take elites).
[*][b]Shops pay a premium for core cards[/b]; every bot gets its chest reward (SPOILS_MAP cashes out); the Knowledge Demon curse and the reward-screen freeze are fixed.
[/list]
[url=https://github.com/imbalilang/STS2-Co-op_Bots/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md]Full changelog[/url]

[h2]AI tiers[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Flash[/b]: 0.5 s/card, budget x0.5
[*][b]Pro[/b]: 1.5 s/card, budget x1.5
[*][b]Cheated[/b]: Pro pacing, 3x gold
[*]All three run the same complete algorithm; only pacing and budget differ.
[/list]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Joint team decisions: shared Vulnerable / Weak, per-member incoming damage, block projection, kill callouts
[*]Combat search on a [b]real simulation[/b] (CombatSolver kernel), not a heuristic score
[*]Deck building by distance from the target build: rewards, shop and removals share one objective, skipping is real
[*]Combat, rewards, shop, events, camp and route advice are all handled by the AI
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Only the host runs the full logic; clients sync the results.
[*]Bots act during the human turn and finish on their own once every human has ended.
[*]Bots have fixed names (Bot 1/2/3).
[*]Isolated build, verified live in multiplayer on the real client; no win-rate claim.
[/list]
